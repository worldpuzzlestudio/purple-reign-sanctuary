About the Founder
The Queen behind Purple Reign
Opal McKnight-Kleinsasser
Founder · Thrice Breast Cancer Overcomer
Purple Reign was not born in a boardroom. It was born in a hospital room — mine.

My name is Opal McKnight-Kleinsasser, and I am a thrice breast cancer overcomer. Three times, I faced the diagnosis that changes everything. Three times, I chose faith over fear. Three times, God brought me through.

But what I remember most from those seasons was not the treatments or the surgeries — it was the loneliness. The 3am moments when fear crept in. The days when I needed someone who truly understood what it felt like to navigate life with a diagnosis that touches every part of you — your body, your mind, your spirit, your family, and your finances.

Because cancer does not just attack your health. It attacks your livelihood, your savings, your ability to work, your plans for the future. The financial weight of a diagnosis is real, and too often it goes unspoken.

Purple Reign was built for those moments. This sanctuary was created so that no woman has to face her journey alone. Whether you are newly diagnosed, deep in treatment, recovering, or celebrating life as an overcomer — there is a place here for you.

You are not a patient. You are a Queen. And Queens reign — even through the storm.
With love and faith, Opal 👑
