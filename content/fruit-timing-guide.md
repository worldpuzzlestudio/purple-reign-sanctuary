# Fruit: A Sweet, Simple Part of Healing

You may have heard that fruit has to be eaten alone or on an empty stomach or it won't "count." That's a myth — your body digests fruit just fine alongside other food. The truth is simpler: fruit is good for you any time of day.

If you want one small tip, mornings have a slight edge for keeping blood sugar steady, and pairing fruit with a little protein or a handful of nuts helps even more. But there's no wrong time to reach for fruit — the goal is just enjoying a variety of it regularly.

## A Few Fruits Worth Knowing

**Watermelon** — About 92% water, making it one of the most hydrating fruits available, plus a good source of lycopene, an antioxidant linked to heart and skin health.

**Papaya** — Low in calories and rich in fiber and vitamins A, C, and E. Contains digestive enzymes (papain and chymopapain) that support healthy digestion.

**Pineapple** — High in vitamin C and manganese, both important for immune function and bone health.

**Apple** — Contains pectin, a fiber that supports digestion and helps with feeling full, plus antioxidants like quercetin.

**Kiwi** — Small but nutrient-dense, offering vitamin C and actinidin, an enzyme that can aid protein digestion.

**Banana** — A strong source of potassium, which supports heart and muscle function, along with quick-digesting natural carbohydrates.

**Pear** — Provides vitamin C, vitamin K, potassium, and copper, along with fiber that supports fullness and digestion.
