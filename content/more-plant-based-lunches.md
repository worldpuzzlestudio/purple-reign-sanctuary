# More Plant-Based Lunch Ideas

## Quinoa Salad Jars

Cook 1 cup rinsed quinoa in 2 cups water — bring to a boil, simmer uncovered 15 minutes until the water's absorbed, then let it steam off-heat for 5 minutes. Separately, cook black beans (soaked overnight, then boiled until soft). In a mason jar, layer the quinoa, beans, corn, chopped cucumber, cherry tomatoes, bell pepper, and green onion. Top with a lemon-tahini dressing or your favorite, seal, and refrigerate until you're ready to grab it and go.

## Sweet Potato Shepherd's Pie

**For the topping:** Boil 3 large sweet potatoes and mash them with 1 cup grated raw pumpkin until creamy (add a splash of water if needed).

**For the filling:** Heat 2 tbsp coconut oil and sauté chopped onion, carrot, celery, and minced garlic briefly. Stir in ½ cup coconut milk and bring to a boil, then add 2 cups cooked lentils along with sage, cumin, bay leaves, oregano, coriander, and salt. Simmer 15 minutes, then stir in 2 tbsp molasses and a dash of cayenne. Simmer another 15 minutes. (A tablespoon of arrowroot powder can thicken the sauce if needed.)

**To assemble:** Grease a baking dish with coconut oil. Layer half the mashed sweet potato/pumpkin on the bottom, add the lentil filling, then top with the remaining raw grated pumpkin and mashed potato. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.

## Simple Hummus

Blend 2 cups cooked chickpeas, ¼ cup tahini, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive or coconut oil, scallion, garlic, cumin, a pinch of cayenne, and basil in a food processor for 2–3 minutes until smooth. Serve with raw veggies or whole grain bread.

## Mason Jar Lentil Soup

Layer dried lentils, diced onion, minced garlic, chopped carrots, diced tomatoes, and vegetable broth in a mason jar with thyme, oregano, and a bay leaf. Store in a cool, dry place. When ready to eat, pour into a pot, bring to a boil, and simmer 25 minutes until tender. Keep warm in a thermos for lunch on the go.

## Rainbow Hummus Veggie Wraps

Spread hummus on a whole-grain wrap, layer with thinly sliced bell peppers, steamed carrots, cucumber, avocado, and shredded purple cabbage, then add mixed greens. Roll tightly, slice, and pack.

## Quinoa-Stuffed Bell Peppers

Mix cooked quinoa with sautéed onion and garlic, diced tomatoes, black beans, corn, and herbs of your choice. Stuff into halved, seeded bell peppers and bake at 375°F for 25–30 minutes until tender. Let cool before packing.

## Mixed Legume Loaf

Blend 3 cups cooked lentils or chickpeas with ground flaxseed, onion powder, garlic powder, turmeric, rolled oats, garlic, herbs, and arrowroot flour (dissolved in a little water) for 2 minutes. Press into a loaf dish and bake 15–20 minutes. Blend tomatoes, onion, cayenne, and salt into a quick sauce, pour over the top, and bake another 10–15 minutes.

## Why Prep Lunches at Home

- **You control what goes in** — more room for fresh, whole ingredients and fewer additives
- **Easy to customize** — adjust for your own tastes, allergies, or dietary needs
- **Saves money** — buying and batch-cooking beats eating out regularly
- **Saves time overall** — a bit of weekend or evening prep means grab-and-go lunches all week
- **Supports steadier energy and focus** — homemade meals mean less reliance on processed convenience food
