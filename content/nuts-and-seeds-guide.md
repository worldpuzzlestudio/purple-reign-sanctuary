# Nuts and Seeds: Small Packages, Big Nourishment

In Exodus 16, God fed the Israelites in the wilderness for forty years with manna — a seed-like bread that appeared each morning, described as resembling coriander seed. It's a reminder that some of the most sustaining foods come in the smallest packages — a fitting way to think about nuts and seeds today.

## Seeds Worth Adding to Your Day

- **Flaxseed** — rich in omega-3s, fiber, and lignans; best ground and added to smoothies, oatmeal, or baked goods
- **Sesame seeds** — excellent source of calcium and iron; toast and sprinkle on veggies, or enjoy as tahini
- **Chia seeds** — high in fiber, omega-3s, and antioxidants; perfect for chia pudding or stirred into yogurt
- **Sunflower seeds** — good source of vitamin E and selenium; great on salads or blended into granola
- **Pumpkin seeds** — rich in zinc and magnesium; roast for a crunchy snack or add to trail mix
- **Hemp seeds** — a complete protein with anti-inflammatory omega fats; sprinkle on toast, oatmeal, or smoothies

## Nuts Worth Knowing

- **Almonds** — vitamin E, calcium, and healthy fats; great in salads, smoothies, or as almond butter
- **Walnuts** — one of the best plant sources of omega-3s and antioxidants
- **Cashews** — creamy monounsaturated fats and protein; ideal for dairy-free sauces and dressings
- **Pistachios** — protein, fiber, and potassium in a satisfying snack
- **Brazil nuts** — exceptionally high in selenium, important for thyroid health
- **Pecans** — rich in antioxidants, especially vitamin E
- **Hazelnuts** — vitamin E and folate; wonderful in nut milk or chopped over grain bowls
- **Macadamia nuts** — heart-healthy monounsaturated fats; lovely blended into creamy sauces
- **Pine nuts** — healthy fats and iron; toast them for salads, pasta, or homemade pesto

## Easy Ways to Use Them

Grinding nuts and seeds is one of the simplest ways to get the most from them — add to smoothies, oatmeal, or baked goods. Beyond that: sprinkle over salads and roasted vegetables, stir into plant-based yogurt, blend into creamy sauces and dressings, or simply enjoy a handful as a satisfying snack.
