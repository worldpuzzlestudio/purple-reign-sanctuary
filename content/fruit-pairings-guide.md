# Fun Fruit Pairings

Mixing fruits isn't just about taste — the right combos make snacks and smoothies more satisfying and interesting. A few easy pairings to try:

- **Berries + apples** — classic, cozy combo, great in a crumble or oatmeal topping
- **Kiwi + pineapple** — bright, tangy duo for smoothies
- **Mango + lime** — sweet and citrusy, perfect for a quick chutney or salsa
- **Berries + banana** — smooth and naturally sweet, a smoothie favorite
- **Pears + grapes** — light and refreshing in a fruit salad
- **Banana + papaya** — soft, tropical, and satisfying

**A couple of tips:**
- **Mix textures** — pair something soft (berries, banana) with something crisp (apple) for more interest in each bite.
- **Go seasonal** — fruit tastes best (and costs less) in season, so let what's fresh guide your combos.

There's no wrong way to combine fruit — this is just inspiration. Trust your taste buds.
