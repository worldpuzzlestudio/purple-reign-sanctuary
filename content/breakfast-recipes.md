# Plant-Based Breakfast Ideas

## Pumpkin Millet Porridge

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup dry millet
- 2 tbsp coconut sugar (for soaking)
- Fresh water for cooking
- Pinch of sea salt
- 1 Ceylon cinnamon stick
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup pumpkin, diced
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 cup coconut milk, divided
- Raw honey or maple syrup, to taste
- Optional: raw cashews, pumpkin seeds, or sunflower seeds

**Directions:**
1. Soak the millet overnight in water with the coconut sugar.
2. When you're ready to cook, pour off the soaking water and add fresh water. Bring to a boil with the salt, cinnamon stick, vanilla, and diced pumpkin.
3. Reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes, stirring now and then.
4. Stir in ½ cup of the coconut milk. Take the pumpkin out and mash it with a fork or potato masher.
5. Return the mashed pumpkin to the pot along with the coriander, then add the rest of the coconut milk. Simmer another 10 minutes.
6. Sweeten with honey or maple syrup to taste, and top with cashews or seeds if you like.

## Sweet Quinoa Balls

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- ¼ cup ground flaxseed
- ¼ cup ground sesame seeds
- ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
- ¼ cup shredded coconut
- Pinch of sea salt
- 2 tbsp honey
- ¼ cup chopped raisins **or** ¼ cup dates

**Directions:**
1. Add everything to a food processor and pulse until smooth.
2. Pour the mixture onto a flat surface lined with parchment paper.
3. Roll the mixture into balls and enjoy them right away, or chill for 20-30 minutes to firm them up.
4. Store any leftover balls in a closed plastic container lined with parchment paper in the fridge. They keep for up to a week.

## Pineapple-Stuffed Sweet Potatoes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 sweet potatoes, washed
- 2 tbsp coconut or almond milk
- 1½ tbsp honey
- ¼ tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp sea salt
- ¼ cup fresh pineapple, cubed
- 1 tsp chopped walnuts or almonds
- 2 tbsp shredded coconut, for topping

**Directions:**
1. Bake the sweet potatoes at 400°F for 50-55 minutes, until very soft. Let them cool slightly, then slice in half.
2. Carefully scoop out the cooked potato with a regular soup spoon, leaving a thin shell.
3. Using a fork, mash the potato with the nut milk until smooth. Stir in the honey, coriander, salt, and pineapple.
4. Using the same soup spoon, fill the potato shells with the mixture. Top with the walnuts or almonds.
5. Bake another 10-15 minutes, until the mixture is warm to the touch.
6. Sprinkle the shredded coconut over the top once they're out of the oven.

## Chia Breakfast Pudding

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 2 tbsp grated coconut
- 4 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- Pinch of grated nutmeg
- Optional: 1 tbsp raw honey or maple syrup

**Toppings (optional):** almond butter, fresh mint, toasted coconut flakes, lime zest, bee pollen, chopped nuts, edible flowers

**Directions:**
1. Whisk the coconut milk, vanilla, and sweetener (if using) until smooth.
2. Stir in the chia seeds and grated coconut.
3. Cover and refrigerate. As the liquid thickens, add a pinch of grated nutmeg.
4. Once the chia seeds have absorbed the liquid, top as you like and enjoy.
