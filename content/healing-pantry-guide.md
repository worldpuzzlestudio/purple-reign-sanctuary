# The Healing Pantry: Plant-Based Staples for Recovery

Food is one of the quiet ways we rebuild — not a cure, but a companion to healing. Here's how to stock your kitchen with the plant-based basics that support the body, whether you're navigating breast cancer recovery or simply choosing a healthier way to eat.

## Pantry Staples

**Whole Grains**
Your grain shelf is where fiber and steady energy live. Beyond the familiar quinoa, brown rice, and oats, branch out into barley, millet, farro, buckwheat, spelt, sorghum, wild rice, amaranth, teff, bulgur, and kamut. Rotating through these keeps meals interesting and covers a wider range of nutrients than sticking to one or two ever could.

**Legumes**
Lentils, chickpeas, and black beans are the workhorses of a plant-based kitchen — protein, fiber, and versatility in one. Keep a broader rotation on hand too: red, green, and French lentils; split peas; kidney, navy, pinto, cannellini, black-eyed, adzuki, mung, and lima beans; fava beans. They carry soups, stews, curries, salads, and burgers equally well.

**Nuts & Seeds**
Almonds, walnuts, chia, and flax are the starting point — add pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, sesame seeds, hemp hearts, pecans, pistachios, cashews, hazelnuts, macadamias, Brazil nuts, pine nuts, and tahini for a fuller range of healthy fats, protein, and minerals.

**Herbs & Spices**
This is where flavor does the heavy lifting so you're not reaching for salt or sugar. Build a rotation of garlic, onion, turmeric, cumin, paprika, ginger, cardamom, coriander, dill, sage, thyme, rosemary, oregano, tarragon, saffron, bay leaves, cilantro, mint, basil, and parsley. A well-spiced pantry makes plant-based eating feel abundant, not restrictive.

## Keeping the Fridge Ready

- **Fruit:** apples, bananas, berries, citrus, grapes, kiwi, mango, watermelon, soursop
- **Vegetables:** leafy greens, cruciferous veggies (broccoli, cauliflower), root vegetables (carrots, beets, potatoes), peppers, tomatoes, cucumber
- **Fresh herbs & aromatics:** garlic, onion, scallion, ginger, parsley, cilantro, basil, celery, turmeric, oregano, dill
- **Plant-based protein:** tofu, cooked lentils, beans, chickpeas
- **Dairy alternatives:** almond milk, soy milk, coconut milk, nut-based cheese and butter
- **Nuts & seeds:** almonds, walnuts, cashews, chia, flax, hemp seeds

*Start with what's familiar, then add one new item a week. A pantry doesn't need to be perfect to support healing — it just needs to be full of real food.*
